# Education icons

These marks identify the universities in Edward Fu’s education history. Files are stored locally and displayed without recoloring, distortion, or cropping.

- `penn-shield.svg`: [Penn shield on Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Shield_of_the_University_of_Pennsylvania.svg), sourced there from Penn’s identity materials; the file page lists it as public domain in the United States. [Penn brand reference](https://branding.web-resources.upenn.edu/logos-and-branding/download-penn-logos).
- `usc-monogram.png`: [USC academic monogram](https://identity.usc.edu/wp-content/uploads/2022/08/TheMonogram.png), from [USC Marks and Logotypes](https://identity.usc.edu/identity/logos-marks/).
- `uci-seal.png`: [UC Irvine simplified two-color seal](https://brand.uci.edu/identity/_img/graphics/uci18_simpleseal_blue_yellow_white.png), from [UC Irvine Marks and Support Graphics](https://brand.uci.edu/identity/graphics.php). This is the simplified digital version.

University names and marks belong to their respective institutions.
