Everyone wants to be heard, and I am here to listen.

Hi, I’m Edward—a product manager, UX researcher, and problem solver passionate about building AI products that help people solve practical problems.

From an early age, I was taught to listen more than I speak. That mindset shapes how I build products today. Listening with curiosity helps me uncover needs people may not express directly, understand their challenges, and turn those insights into thoughtful product decisions.

I bring everything I’ve learned across research, design, business, and technology into my work. This combination helps me connect customer needs with business goals, make complex workflows easier to understand, and carry ideas through development and launch.

I’m especially excited about making AI understandable and useful. I use customer feedback to guide what I build and AI tools to prototype and iterate, always keeping the people using the product at the center.

I’m open to relocation and opportunities in product management and AI.
